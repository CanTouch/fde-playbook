## Phase 2 (minutes 5-12): theater and exceptions
Ask each of the six questions and write the answers.
1. Why do you do it this way today?
2. Who really decides?
3. Which step is theater?
4. Which step is legitimate?
5. What happens when there is an exception?
6. How often do exceptions happen?

## Phase 3 (minutes 12-17): baseline numbers
- Time from first message to a quote or answer: ________
- After a quote goes out, days before anyone follows up: ________
- Quotes that never get a follow-up (rough share): ________
- Hours per week spent reading, logging and replying, and whose hours: ________
- Where a real record exists (chat export, CRM, inbox) that I can pull: ________

## Phase 4 (minutes 17-20): outcome and dates
Say: "We build inside the tools you already use, and you stay the person who approves. Who owns this process, who is the sponsor, and when do we re-measure?"

- Owner agreed: yes / no
- Sponsor agreed: yes / no
- Smallest first step (one workflow, one removed step): ________________
- Data access needed and by when: ________________
- Re-measure dates (day 30 and day 90 for small business, month 3 and 6 for enterprise): ________

## After the call
- Send a summary within a day, with the numbers you were given and what you will pull yourself.
- Do not quote a price until you have a baseline, unless you are offering the free first project.
