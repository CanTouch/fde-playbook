# Pitching and objections

## Who to pitch [Source]
| Buyer | Cares about | Opening question |
|---|---|---|
| CFO | Cost, margin, accuracy, month-end close | How long does it take you to close your books? |
| Sales lead | Quotes sent, deals closed, cycle time | How many quotes go out and never get a follow-up? |
| People lead | Hiring faster, onboarding, leverage | How long until a new hire is productive? |
| IT lead | Output, modernization, working inside current systems | Which systems must this live inside? |
| Small-business owner [Adapted] | Lost jobs, evenings spent on messages, getting paid | When a quote goes out, how many days before you chase it? |

Frame the result as reallocating people. The agent removes the administrative load, and the strongest staff move to higher-leverage work.

## Objections [Adapted]
**"We tried AI and it did nothing."**
It was probably aimed at the documented process. We map the real one first and measure before we build.

**"Will this replace my staff?"**
It removes the typing and chasing. A person still decides prices and promises, and spends the saved time on work that needs them.

**"We do not have time."**
You need two short interviews and one owner who approves the numbers. We do the rest.

**"Can't we just buy a tool?"**
You can, and it still needs mapping to your process. We start from the map and use tools you already have where we can.

**"What if it sends something wrong?"**
Nothing involving money or a promise goes out without your approval. Drafts wait for you in the app you already use.

**"What does it cost to run?"**
We write the monthly running cost under each step of the map, so you see it before you agree.
