# Outreach templates

The source material describes the approach (offer one simple workflow, end to end, on a set timeline, for free if you have no track record) but gives no word-for-word script. These are suggestions to adapt. They are untested, so change the wording to match your voice and measure what gets replies.

## First message
Hi [Name]. I help [trade] businesses stop losing quotes that go quiet. I would like to map one workflow with you, from the first enquiry to the quote follow-up, and build a fix for it at no charge. In return I would ask for your numbers before and after, and permission to write it up. It takes two short conversations from you. Worth 20 minutes this week?

## Follow-up after four days
Hi [Name]. Quick follow-up on my note about quotes that go quiet. If it helps, I can send a one-page list of the five questions I would ask, so you can see what the 20 minutes covers. Happy to drop it if the timing is wrong.

## Last message
Hi [Name]. I will stop here so I do not clutter your inbox. If lost follow-ups ever become a priority, reply with "quotes" and I will pick it up from there.

## Habit
Pick one day a week for outreach. The practice routine in the source ends the week with outreach to small businesses on Friday.

## Before you send
- Name one specific thing about their business (a service, a recent job, a review).
- Check local rules on cold messaging: email consent, SMS and WhatsApp opt-in, and data protection law in their country.
