FDE Engagement Flow

From a new lead to a measured case study. Diamonds are decisions. Every "no" has a defined next move.

Plain code Agent Human decides Stop or loop back Milestone 
flowchart TD
  A(["New lead"]):::ms --> B["20-minute discovery call"]
  B --> C{"One process with a named owner and sponsor?"}
  C -- No --> C2["Narrow to one process, or walk away"]:::stop
  C2 --> C
  C -- Yes --> D["1. Map the real process: interviews, systems, documents"]
  D --> E["2. Measure elapsed time vs touch time, count idle days"]
  E --> F["3. Sort every step"]
  F --> F1["Not needed: Delete"]:::stop
  F --> F2["Fixed rule: Plain code"]:::code
  F --> F3["Needs judgment: Agent drafts"]:::agent
  F --> F4["Costly mistake: Human decides"]:::human
  F1 --> G
  F2 --> G
  F3 --> G
  F4 --> G
  G{"4. Baseline signed off by the owner?"}
  G -- No --> G2["Pull real timestamps and exports"]:::stop
  G2 --> G
  G -- Yes --> H["5. Pitch the outcome and price it"]
  H --> I{"Client agrees?"}
  I -- No --> I2["Offer a free or low-cost first project, or stop"]:::stop
  I2 --> H
  I -- Yes --> J["6. Build inside the client's own tools"]:::code
  J --> K["Agent prepares, person approves anything involving money or promises"]:::human
  K --> L(["Go live on real traffic"]):::ms
  L --> M["7. Re-measure the same metrics the same way: day 30 small business, month 3 and 6 enterprise"]
  M --> N{"Metrics improved against baseline?"}
  N -- No --> N2["Fix exceptions, adjust the sort, re-measure"]:::stop
  N2 --> M
  N -- Yes --> O["8. Write the case study: before map, after map, owner quote, running cost"]:::ms
  O --> P(["Price the next project from the measured result"]):::ms

Numbers 1 to 8 match the stages in the FDE Playbook. The flow is the same for a small business and an enterprise. What changes is the timeline: 30 days and a day-90 check for a small shop, 8 weeks and 3 and 6 month audits for an enterprise.
