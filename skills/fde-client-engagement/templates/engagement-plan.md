# Engagement plans

Put dates on everything in the first meeting, including the re-measurement.

## Small business: 30 days (adaptation)

| Week | Do | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Three real cases, export chat and email history, draw the real map, fill the baseline table, owner initials the numbers | Baseline signed |
| 2 | Sort the steps, build the smallest version (capture, log, timers) | Leads are captured and logged automatically |
| 3 | Add the agent that drafts replies, with the owner approving before anything is sent. Run on live traffic | Owner approves drafts from their normal app |
| 4 | Fix exceptions, write a short handover, re-measure at day 30 | Day-30 table filled |

Also book a day-90 check before you start.

## Enterprise: 8 weeks plus audits (from the source)

| Period | Do |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1-4 | Audit: human interviews, 3 to 4 weeks of process mining on the system of record, document review. Baseline all KPIs, select process owners, build proofs of concept |
| Weeks 5-8 | Build, integrate and deploy production agents inside the client's systems |
| Month 3 and month 6 | Audit against the baseline and show the numbers to the executive sponsor |

## Checkpoints for both
- Owner and sponsor named in writing.
- Baseline signed before any build.
- Approvals routed to the owner in the tool they already use.
- Monthly running cost written down.
- Re-measure dates in the calendar.
